# Hi-res category source artwork

The numbered video thumbnails are downloaded originals, not enlarged launcher IFFs.
All launcher outputs were fitted once from the listed source into a black 404x280 canvas, quantized to at most 240 colors, and encoded with ByteRun1 RLE.
Med-res and ECS outputs were not updated.

## Downloaded sources

- amiga-addict-11.jpg: 905x1280, https://amiga.simulant.media/issue-11-is-on-sale-now-with-our-thea500-mini-review/
- kimjustice_top100.jpg: 1280x720, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tWQfX-hP1RQ
- amigamejam-banner.png: 960x240, https://itch.io/jam/amigamejam2025
- kimjustice_platformers.jpg: 1280x720, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RKmBIUx3-x4
- kimjustice_psygnosis.jpg: 1280x720, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9a85IeA888
- pixelthing_45games.jpg: 1280x720, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mg8IYHJnyvY
- pixelthing_23exclusive.jpg: 1280x720, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EjyYe8IL0Io

The AmiGameJam banner is cropped to (242, 14, 708, 140) to retain its logo and subtitle while omitting sponsors.

## Retained originals

Other categories use their existing original PNG/JPEG files in the parent directory, rather than the old pre-rendered IFFs. EAB, Lemon Amiga and v's Recommended Games remain limited by their small original logos; they were kept at native size rather than upscaled. Retro Gamer uses its existing 350x453 cover, which is larger than the fitted portrait region (216x280).

## Original site assets checked directly

- eab-original.gif: 309x60, downloaded from https://eab.abime.net/gfx/eab.gif
- lemon-original.gif: 118x54, downloaded from https://www.lemonamiga.com/assets/amiga/images/navigation/signs/logo.gif
- vgames-site.png: 160x160, downloaded from https://www.gametechwiki.com/logos/Wiki_logo_135.png

These are the assets actually served by the current sites. They are smaller than the existing repository artwork and were retained as source references, not installed as replacements.

Amiga Addict Top 100 now uses user-supplied `amiga-addict-100.png`, centered with aspect ratio preserved on a 404x280 canvas. Side padding is the median source edge color, RGB (206, 212, 216). The issue 11 cover is retained as an unused source reference.
